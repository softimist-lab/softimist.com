## About Softimist Limited

Softimist Limited is an EdTech and Video Platform Solution provider based in Bangladesh. We build scalable solutions including Learning Management Systems (LMS), Video on Demand (VOD) platforms, serving clients in Bangladesh and internationally.

## About the Role

We're looking for enthusiastic **Fresher or Junior SQA Engineers** who are passionate about software quality, testing, and ensuring great user experiences. You'll work closely with our engineering and product teams to test modern web applications, identify issues, and help deliver stable, high-quality releases.

## Job Responsibilities

- **Perform manual testing** of web applications
- **Write and execute test cases**, test plans, and bug reports
- **Identify, document, and track bugs** using issue tracking tools (e.g., Jira or similar)
- **Collaborate with developers** to ensure high-quality releases
- **Perform regression, smoke, and functional testing**
- Basic understanding of **API testing** is a plus

## Requirements

- Basic understanding of **software testing principles** and QA lifecycle
- Familiarity with **web applications** and how they work
- Good **analytical and problem-solving skills**
- **Attention to detail** and strong communication skills
- Knowledge of **testing tools** (Postman, Jira, or similar) is a plus
- Understanding of **SDLC/STLC** is preferred but not mandatory for freshers
